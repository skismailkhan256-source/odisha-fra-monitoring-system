About the FRA Monitoring System

The Forest Rights Act (FRA), 2006 recognizes the rights of forest-dwelling Scheduled Tribes and other traditional forest dwellers over forest land and resources.

This platform brings together data processing, exploratory data analysis, machine learning clustering and WebGIS visualization to support analytical monitoring of FRA implementation across Odisha.

Platform Objective: To transform FRA-related data into meaningful analytical insights that can help identify geographical patterns, beneficiary distribution and areas requiring further monitoring.
